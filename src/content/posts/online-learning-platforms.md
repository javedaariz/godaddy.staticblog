## 1. Coursera

Courses and certificates from universities and companies across technology, business and health.

## 2. Udemy

A large library of affordable, self-paced courses on coding, design, marketing and more.

## 3. LinkedIn Learning

Professional development courses that link to your LinkedIn profile.

## 4. edX

University-level courses and microcredentials from leading institutions.

## 5. Skillshare

Creative, project-based classes in design, photography, writing and more.

## 6. Khan Academy

Free lessons in maths, science and economics, good for foundations.

## 7. Pluralsight

Technology-focused learning paths and skill assessments.

## 8. FutureLearn

Courses and microcredentials with a focus on interaction and discussion.

## 9. Codecademy

Interactive lessons in programming, web development and data science.

## 10. Udacity

Project-based programmes in technology, AI and data, with mentor support.

## Before you pay

Prices, free trials and certificates change often, so check each provider's current terms. If you are self-employed, some learning costs can be a business expense. Confirm the rules on GOV.UK or with an accountant.
