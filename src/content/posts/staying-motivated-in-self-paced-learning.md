## 1. Set clear goals

Define a specific, measurable aim, such as "finish module three by Friday". Vague goals are easy to drop.

## 2. Break content into chunks

Short lessons feel manageable and help you remember more.

## 3. Build a routine

Block out regular time each day or week. Consistency matters more than long sessions.

## 4. Track progress

Use a checklist or journal. Seeing progress builds momentum.

## 5. Reward milestones

Celebrate when you finish a module. Small rewards keep effort going.

## 6. Remove distractions

Silence notifications and choose a quiet space.

## 7. Join a community

Forums, study groups and discussion boards add accountability.

## 8. Apply what you learn

Use each lesson on a real project, such as a client piece of work. Practice makes it stick.

## 9. Reflect and adjust

Review what is working every few weeks and change your approach if it is not.

## 10. Stay inspired

Remind yourself why you started, and follow people who do the work you want to do. See our overview of [online learning platforms](/learn/online-learning-platforms/).
