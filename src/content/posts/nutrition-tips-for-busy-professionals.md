## 1. Start with a balanced breakfast

Include protein and fibre, for example eggs on wholegrain toast or yoghurt with fruit and oats. It helps you avoid mid-morning cravings.

## 2. Plan meals ahead

Batch cooking on a quiet day means a healthy option is ready when you are busy.

## 3. Keep healthy snacks to hand

Nuts, fruit and yoghurt are quick. Processed snacks tend to lead to energy dips.

## 4. Drink water through the day

Keep a bottle at your desk and refill it regularly.

## 5. Have a proper lunch

Include protein, vegetables and wholegrains. Skipping lunch often leads to overeating later.

## 6. Watch caffeine and sugar

They give a short lift followed by a dip. Moderate your intake, especially in the afternoon.

## 7. Get fibre and protein

Fibre keeps you full and supports digestion. Protein helps steady your energy.

## 8. Eat mindfully

Step away from your screen when you eat. You will usually enjoy it more and eat more sensibly.

## 9. Use meal deliveries wisely

Meal kits can save time. Check the ingredients and portion sizes before you commit to a subscription.

## 10. Check in with yourself

Notice how you feel after different meals and adjust. For personal advice, such as for a medical condition, speak to your GP or a registered dietitian.
