## 1. Task management

Todoist, Asana and Trello track tasks, deadlines and priorities. Tags, boards and recurring tasks keep things organised.

## 2. Calendar and scheduling

Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook and Calendly handle scheduling and reminders.

## 3. Communication

Slack, Microsoft Teams and Zoom cover chat and meetings, especially for remote and hybrid work.

## 4. Notes and knowledge

Notion, Evernote and OneNote give you one place for notes, ideas and documentation.

## 5. Cloud storage

Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive store and share files safely.

## 6. Focus and time tracking

Pomodoro timers, Forest and Toggl help you focus and see where your time goes. This is useful for freelancers who bill by the hour.

## 7. Automation

Zapier and IFTTT connect apps and remove repetitive work.

## 8. Password management

1Password, Bitwarden and similar tools keep logins secure. Use one rather than reusing passwords.

## 9. Project management suites

Monday.com and ClickUp combine tasks, timelines and reporting for larger projects.

## 10. Learning

LinkedIn Learning, Coursera and Udemy help you keep your skills current. See our [guide to online learning platforms](/learn/online-learning-platforms/).
