## 1. Move in the morning

A short walk or light stretching gets you going. Even ten minutes counts.

## 2. Eat a balanced diet

Build meals around vegetables, fruit, whole grains and protein. Eating consistently well matters more than strict diets.

## 3. Drink enough fluid

Keep water within reach during the day. Thirst, headaches and tiredness can all be signs you need more.

## 4. Manage stress

Short breaks, deep breathing or meditation can help. If stress is affecting your daily life, speak to your GP.

## 5. Keep a regular sleep routine

The NHS says most adults need around 6 to 9 hours a night. Going to bed and getting up at similar times helps.

## 6. Exercise regularly

Mix cardio, strength and flexibility work. Choose something you will actually keep doing. The NHS publishes weekly activity guidelines for adults on its Live Well pages.

## 7. Limit highly processed food

Cut back on foods high in sugar, salt and saturated fat, and choose whole foods more often.

## 8. Stay connected

Time with friends, family and your community supports mood and motivation. Remote workers should plan this in on purpose.

## 9. Attend checkups

Go to dental appointments and the NHS screening you are invited to. Finding problems early makes them easier to treat.

## 10. Set realistic goals

Pick one habit, make it easy, and build from there. Small steady changes last.
