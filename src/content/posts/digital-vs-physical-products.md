## Physical products

Physical products are tangible items that need stock, storage, packaging and shipping, such as clothing, books and homeware.

**Pros**
- Customers value things they can hold.
- Packaging offers a way to build a premium brand.
- You can offer variety in size, colour and edition.

**Cons**
- Stock and storage cost money.
- Shipping can be slow and expensive.
- Returns and damage add complexity.

## Digital products

Digital products are delivered electronically, such as ebooks, templates, software and online courses.

**Pros**
- Low overheads, with no stock or shipping.
- You can sell unlimited copies at little extra cost.
- Delivery is instant.

**Cons**
- Higher risk of unauthorised sharing.
- You need the skills to create and distribute them.
- It can be harder to show the value of something intangible.

## Choosing

Consider your audience, your expertise, how much logistics you can handle and your profit goals. Many sellers combine both. Whichever you choose, read our guide on [how online stores build customer trust](/freelancer-finance/how-online-stores-build-customer-trust/).
