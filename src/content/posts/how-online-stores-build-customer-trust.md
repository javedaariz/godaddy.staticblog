## 1. Clear product information

Detailed descriptions, good photos and specifications help people buy with confidence.

## 2. Secure payment

Use trusted payment providers and make it obvious that checkout is secure.

## 3. Transparent pricing and policies

Show the full price, delivery costs and returns policy before checkout. Surprises at the last step lose sales.

## 4. Real reviews

Display genuine customer reviews. Never fake or buy them.

## 5. A clean, responsive site

A fast, tidy site that works on phones signals professionalism.

## 6. Responsive support

Make it easy to reach you and reply quickly.

## 7. Brand and social proof

Tell your story and show real customers and results.

## 8. Reliable delivery

On-time delivery with tracking builds repeat custom.

## 9. Consistency

Keep branding and messages consistent across your website, social media and emails.

## 10. Privacy and compliance

Follow UK GDPR and be clear about how you use customer data. Selling online as a business also has tax and registration rules, so check GOV.UK, or see our [guide to freelancer finances](/freelancer-finance/financial-planning-for-uk-freelancers/).
